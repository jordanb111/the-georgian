The Community

A Little More Georgetown.

The Georgian is 40 apartments on two stories, set around a courtyard pool and the live oaks that shade it.

Out on I-35, new complexes run to hundreds of units, with parking garages, long interior hallways, and a leasing office you visit once. Here, life centers on one courtyard. You learn your neighbors’ names, the trees are older than the building, and Georgetown’s Square is a walk up Austin Avenue instead of a drive.
